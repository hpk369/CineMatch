CineMatch

Hybrid movie recommendation engine built on Hadoop MapReduce. Implements three recommendation approaches — TF-IDF content similarity, co-occurrence collaborative filtering, and score-fusion hybrid — trained on the MovieLens 100K dataset.

How It Works

Each algorithm approaches the recommendation problem from a different angle. Understanding the differences helps you interpret what you see in the demo above.

🎬

Content-Based

Builds a personal genre profile for each user by TF-IDF-weighting every genre they've rated. High-frequency genres get lower weight (like stopwords in NLP) — rare genre preferences count more. Then scores all unrated movies by cosine similarity to that profile. Runs as two MapReduce steps.

What to expect

Results closely reflect the user's genre history. A Drama fan gets Drama-heavy picks. Results are predictable and explainable but may feel repetitive — it won't suggest something outside your usual taste.

TF-IDF · Genre Similarity · 2 MR Steps
👥

Collaborative

Builds a co-occurrence matrix: for every pair of movies rated by the same user, increments their co-occurrence count. Then for each user, scores unrated movies by the total co-occurrence weight with their rated films. Two MapReduce passes: one to build the matrix, one to compute scores.

What to expect

More eclectic and surprising picks. This method doesn't know about genres — it only knows what people watched together. You may see recommendations that cross genre lines but reflect real viewing patterns.

Co-occurrence Matrix · 2 MR Steps
⚡

Hybrid

Multiplies the content-based and collaborative scores for each (user, movie) pair. A movie must score well on both signals to rank here — pure genre matches that aren't popular in co-occurrence get filtered out, and vice versa. One final MapReduce step joins and multiplies the two output files.

What to expect

The strongest, most balanced recommendations. These movies fit your genre taste AND align with crowd wisdom. Only available for users who have scores in both methods — try users 1–40 for best results.

Score Fusion · Multiplication

Architecture

All processing runs as Python MapReduce jobs via mrjob, executed on a Hadoop cluster (HDFS + YARN). The pipeline is fully parallelisable across the cluster.
